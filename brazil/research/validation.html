Methodological boundary

The model is the fixed-weight production ensemble. Every forecast is fitted only on preceding quarters after an initial 36-quarter training sample. COVID is 2020-Q1 through 2021-Q4; Post-COVID starts in 2022-Q1. This is pseudo-OOS using today’s revised data and factor space; it is not a real-time vintage reconstruction.
