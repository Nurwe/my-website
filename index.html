January 2026 - Bachelor Thesis

Fiscal Balance and Economic Growth: A Relationship Conditioned by Sovereign Risk

This thesis studies how government spending, tax revenue, and sovereign risk interact in Argentina, a country marked by repeated fiscal imbalance and credibility problems. It uses a Structural VAR with Blanchard-Perotti identification and received a grade of 6/6.
