May 2026 - Bachelor Thesis

Fiscal Balance and Economic Growth: A Relationship Conditioned by Sovereign Risk

This thesis studies how government spending, tax revenue, and sovereign risk interact in Argentina, a country marked by repeated fiscal imbalance and credibility problems. Using a Structural Vector Autoregressive model, it finds that spending shocks have an insignificant effect on output while tax shocks are associated with stronger growth, highlighting the importance of fiscal balance in vulnerable economies.
